Personal statement个人陈述

Mathematics as a way of thinking.以数学作为思考问题的方式。

My path from pure mathematics to applied mathematics, AI research, and industry.

从纯数学到应用数学、AI 研究与业界实践的个人路径。

Background个人经历

Starting from pure mathematics从纯数学出发

I began my academic training in pure mathematics. As an undergraduate, I spent most of my time building foundations across analysis, algebra, geometry, topology, probability, and differential equations. What stayed with me was not only a collection of techniques, but a particular way of working: identify the essential structure, separate assumptions from conclusions, and look for the simplest formulation that explains the problem.

我的学术训练始于纯数学。本科阶段，我把大量时间投入到分析、代数、几何、拓扑、概率和微分方程等基础方向。真正留下来的，不只是具体的数学技巧，更是一种处理问题的方式：辨认最关键的结构，区分假设与结论，并寻找能够解释问题的最简形式。

Moving toward application转向应用数学与 AI

Over time, I became increasingly interested in problems where mathematical ideas interact with data, computation, and physical systems. This led me from pure mathematics toward applied mathematics, scientific machine learning, equation discovery, neural operators, and language models. Even when the work is highly empirical, I tend to begin with the same questions: What is the mathematical object being learned? Which constraints actually matter? Where does the current formulation fail?

随着学习深入，我逐渐对数学思想如何与数据、计算和物理系统发生联系产生了更强的兴趣。这使我从纯数学走向应用数学，并开始关注科学机器学习、方程发现、神经算子和语言模型。即使面对高度经验化的工作，我仍然习惯先问：模型真正学习的数学对象是什么？哪些约束是本质的？现有问题形式又在哪里失效？

Research alongside industry研究与业界实践

After completing the first year of my Ph.D., I chose to work in industry alongside my academic research. Since then, internships have given me a direct view of how AI systems are trained, evaluated, and improved in practice. They also made the gap between an interesting idea and a useful system much more concrete. I value both sides: the depth needed to understand a problem and the discipline needed to make a solution work.

博士第一年结束后，我选择在学术研究之外进入业界实习，并一直延续至今。这些经历让我直接接触到 AI 系统在实际环境中的训练、评估与改进过程，也让我更具体地理解“有趣的想法”和“真正有用的系统”之间的距离。我重视这两方面：既要有理解问题所需的深度，也要有让解决方案真正工作的执行能力。

What I want to pursue我希望继续寻找的方向

I want to work on meaningful problems rather than optimize a metric in isolation. My strongest interest lies in questions where mathematical thinking can reveal a different solution—not simply make an established pipeline slightly larger or more refined. I am especially drawn to work that connects clear structure, ambitious ideas, and practical impact.

我希望参与真正有意义的问题，而不是孤立地优化某一个指标。我最感兴趣的是那些能够通过数学思考得到不同解法的问题，而不仅仅是在既有流程上继续扩大规模或做更精细的打磨。我尤其希望寻找能够同时连接清晰结构、有进取心的想法与实际影响的工作。

For me, mathematics is not a boundary around what I work on. It is the perspective I bring to new problems.

对我而言，数学并不是限定研究对象的边界，而是我面对新问题时所采用的视角。
